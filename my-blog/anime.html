Welcome to Demon School, Iruma-kun by Osamu Nishi

Picture getting sold to demons by your own parents, then somehow becoming the most beloved kid in demon school. Iruma just wants to survive without anyone finding out he's human, but his talent for accidentally becoming a legend keeps getting in the way. It's silly, heartwarming, and way more emotional than a "kid goes to demon school" premise has any right to be.

Rating:★★★★★

Steins;Gate by Chiyomaru Shikura

A microwave, a text message, and one bad decision are all it takes to unravel time itself. What starts as a group of friends messing around with "future gadget" nonsense spirals into a race against fate itself, where every fix creates a worse problem. The slow burn is real, but once it hits, it doesn't let go.

Rating:★★★★★
