Fire Punch by Tatsuki FUjimoto

A boy who can regenerate from anything gets set on fire and just... keeps burning, forever. What follows is one of the most brutal, unpredictable stories about identity, faith, and what's left of a person after everything's been stripped away. Not for the faint of heart, but unforgettable.

Downfall by Inio Asan

A once-celebrated manga artist watches his career, marriage, and sense of self quietly fall apart after one scandal. It's less about big dramatic moments and more about sitting with someone as they hit rock bottom and try to figure out who they even are anymore. Uncomfortable in the best way.
